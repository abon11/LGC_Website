The Format
Friday — Practice round. No scores recorded.
Saturday — Individual stroke play. Both gross and net score count. Gross seeds Sunday teams. Net determines your buy-in.
Sunday — 2-man scramble. Captain & Crew. Best Saturday gross pairs with worst (1&12, 2&11, etc.). Lowest team score wins the pot.
Ground Rules
Lift, clean, and place between the trees.
Play the white tees (or equivalent).
Standing water in a bunker — pull your ball out on the line it went in.
All other bunker conditions are group discretion. If it's bad, free relief (GUR is up to group discretion).
Everything else is played as a hazard (drop on line of entry).
Hazard / rugged terrain: play it as it lies unless there's danger of hitting a root or rock. Move to nearest point of relief, no closer to hole, staying in the same terrain. Free relief.
No gimmes for birdie or better.
Par or worse: gimme if any part of the ball is closer than the start of your putter grip. Place putter head in hole — if the ball is before the grip, it's good.
Have fun — it's just a game.

Team Making
Based on gross scores. Rank 1–12 and pair: 1&12, 2&11, 3&10, 4&9, 5&8, 6&7.
Tiebreakers
Better handicap gets higher seed.
Better front 9 gets higher seed.
Better score on #1 handicap hole, then #2, and so on.
If still tied: kissing rule, then coin flip.
Solo Round Exception
If 10+ players complete a full 18-hole round, up to 2 players who didn't will be placed with a net score one stroke worse than 6th place (locking them into 7th). They pay in normally, and their gross for team ranking is that "fake" net + handicap.
Rain Rules
Friday Rain
Practice round canceled. Move on.
Saturday Rain
If all 12 finished 9+ holes Friday, use Friday scores for the solo round.
If 10+ completed full 18 on Friday, use the solo round exception rule.
If neither: try to reschedule (potential 36 holes Sunday). If not possible, everyone pays the same and the teams are drafted via the handicaps: draft order goes from highest HDCP to lowest HDCP (skipping those already picked). HDCP ties are resolved via a yard closest to pin (winner gets better pick), last year's gross scores (better score gets worse pick), prior year's HDCP, then tiebreakers from the Team Making section on the prior year's results.
Sunday Rain
Try to reschedule for Monday morning (any course).
If Monday not possible: top 2 net scores crowned champions (asterisk on trophy). No payouts — everyone refunded.
If there is a tie in this case, it is resolved via a best of 7 closest to the pin game, followed by the normal Team Making tiebreakers (excluding HDCP).
Both Days Rained Out
If all 12 finished 18 Friday, use Friday as the solo round.
If no valid round exists and no makeup possible, the tournament is canceled. A Beer Dye Tournament will be held instead — winners still get engraved.
Sunday Tiebreakers
If two or more teams are tied at the end of play Sunday, the following tiebreakers will be in effect:
Try to go b ack and play playoff holes (either replay 18, go off on 1, etc). This may result in having to pay a replay fee for 9 holes.
If course is full, search other courses in the area to play 9 holes. This is still the normal format of lose one and you're out.
Come back Monday morning for playoff holes.
If nothing else is possible, best of 7 closest to the pin.
